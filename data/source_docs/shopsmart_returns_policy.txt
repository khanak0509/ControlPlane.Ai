ShopSmart Returns & Refunds Policy (Internal Reference)

ShopSmart accepts returns on most items within 30 days of the delivery date, provided the item is unused, unworn, and in its original packaging where applicable. Apparel must have original tags attached. Opened electronics and small appliances are eligible for return within the same 30-day window but are subject to a 10% restocking fee, deducted from the refund total, unless the item is defective.

Items that arrive damaged or defective are treated as an exception to the standard window: customers have up to 45 days from the delivery date to initiate a return, and these returns are eligible for a full refund, including the original shipping cost, with no restocking fee applied.

Once a returned item is received and inspected at our processing center, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Store credit refunds, when requested in place of a refund to the original payment method, are typically applied within 2 business days of inspection.

Exchanges (size or color swaps on the same item) are processed within 3 business days of the warehouse receiving the original item, and do not incur a restocking fee.

The following items are final sale and not eligible for return under any circumstances: gift cards, personalized or custom-engraved items, and intimate apparel that has been opened.

Customer support agents should always confirm the order number and delivery date before quoting a return window, and should never waive the 45-day defective-item exception or the 10% electronics restocking fee without a supervisor override logged in the case system. Any request to return an item outside these windows should be escalated rather than approved directly.
